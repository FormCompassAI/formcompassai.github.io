Privacy Policy

FormCompass AI · Last updated July 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FormCompass AI ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application and related services (the "Service").

Questions or concerns? Reading this policy will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use the Service. If you have any questions, contact us at support@formcompassai.com.

Summary

  • What we collect: your email address and password (for your account), the exercise videos you submit, and basic device/usage information.
  • Sensitive information: we do not collect sensitive personal information (such as health records, biometric identifiers, race, or religious beliefs).
  • Third-party data: we do not collect information about you from third parties.
  • Why we process it: to create and manage your account, run your video analysis, process payments, provide support, and keep the Service secure.
  • Who we share it with: the service providers listed below, who help us run the app (hosting, AI analysis, payments) — never sold to advertisers or data brokers.
  • Your rights: you can access, correct, or delete your data at any time, including by deleting your account directly in the app.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

When you create an account, we collect your email address and password. We do not collect or store payment card details ourselves — payments are handled entirely by Apple, Google, or RevenueCat (see Section 4).

Information from using the app

If you grant permission, we access your device's camera and photo/video library so you can submit a video for analysis. You can change this permission at any time in your device settings.

We also automatically collect basic device and usage data — such as device model, operating system version, app version, and general diagnostic/crash information — to keep the Service secure and working properly.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account, including signing you in securely.
  • Process your submitted video and deliver your analysis results.
  • Process subscription payments and manage your plan.
  • Respond to support requests and questions.
  • Send you necessary service communications (such as account confirmation emails).
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or security issues.
  • Improve and maintain the Service.

3. Legal Bases for Processing (EU/UK Users)

If you are located in the EU, UK, or Switzerland, we rely on the following legal bases to process your information: your consent (which you can withdraw at any time), the need to perform our contract with you (providing the Service you signed up for), our legitimate business interests (such as keeping the Service secure and improving it), and compliance with legal obligations.

4. Who We Share Information With

We share information only with service providers who help us operate the Service, under contracts that require them to protect your data and use it only as we instruct. We do not sell your personal information.

  • AI analysis providers: Google Cloud AI and OpenAI — process video frames to generate your technique analysis.
  • Cloud infrastructure: Supabase and Railway — host our database, authentication, and backend processing.
  • Video and workflow processing: Make.com and Cloudinary — handle video/image processing pipelines.
  • Payments and billing: Apple (App Store), Google Play Billing, and RevenueCat — process your subscription payments. We never see or store your card details.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of business assets.

5. Artificial Intelligence Use

The Service uses AI/machine learning technology, provided through OpenAI and Google Cloud AI, to analyze the exercise videos you submit and generate your muscle-emphasis ranking and technique feedback. Your video frames and related data are shared with these providers solely to produce your analysis, in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

6. International Data Transfers

Our servers and service providers are located in Ireland and the United States. If you are located in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, your information may be transferred to and processed in these countries, which may not have the same data protection laws as your home country. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses to protect your information in these transfers.

7. How Long We Keep Your Information

We retain your personal information for as long as you have an account with us. When you delete your account, we delete your account information, credits, videos, and analysis results, except where we are required to retain limited data for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes.

8. How We Keep Your Information Safe

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children, and you must be at least 18 years old (or the age of majority in your jurisdiction) to use it. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under this age. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us at support@formcompassai.com and we will take steps to delete it.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can exercise most of these rights directly by deleting your account in the app, or by contacting us at support@formcompassai.com.

If you are in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. If you are in Switzerland, you may contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner.

11. Do Not Track

Our Service does not currently respond to "Do Not Track" browser signals, as there is no accepted industry standard for how to interpret them.

12. US State Privacy Rights

If you are a resident of California or another US state with a comprehensive privacy law, you may have additional rights, including the right to know what personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, obtain a copy of it, opt out of certain processing, and not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

In the preceding 12 months, we have disclosed the following categories of personal information to service providers for business purposes: identifiers (such as your email address), commercial information (such as subscription/payment status), and audio/visual information (the videos you submit for analysis). We have not sold this information.

To exercise your rights, contact us at support@formcompassai.com. We may need to verify your identity before processing your request. If we decline your request, you may appeal by emailing us at the same address.

13. Video and Image Processing

When you submit a video for analysis, it is uploaded to our storage and processed to generate your result. During processing, still frames are extracted from your video and analyzed by third-party services we use, including Railway, Make.com, Cloudinary, and AI providers (OpenAI and Google), to produce your technique feedback. Your video and the images derived from it are used solely to generate your analysis result and are not used for any other purpose.

Your uploaded videos and the images derived from them are retained only as long as necessary to produce and deliver your analysis. You can delete your account at any time from within the app, which deletes your account, your uploaded videos, and the associated processing artifacts from our storage and from the third-party services listed above. Some cached copies held by processing services may persist for a short period before being automatically removed.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
